# AI Judge Preparation

Maintain concise, evidence-backed answers for:

1. What problem does the agent solve?
2. Why is an agent appropriate instead of a deterministic program or one-shot LLM call?
3. Why this model and tool set?
4. What is model-driven and what is deterministic?
5. How are prompt injection and unsafe inputs handled?
6. How are evidence and citations prevented from being hallucinated?
7. How are loops, latency, and cost bounded?
8. How was the system evaluated?
9. What failed during development and what changed?
10. What would change for production?

Record exact implementation details and measured results, not generic AI terminology.
